START HERE

The big ideas

I will model integer addition as directed movement, connect the endpoint to a chip sum, and choose a useful horizontal or vertical line.

First addend is the start

Do not begin at zero unless the first addend is zero.

Second addend is movement

Its sign gives direction and magnitude gives distance.

Endpoint is the sum

The landing position is the combined result.

Models agree

Opposite movements undo just as zero pairs cancel.

LEARN

Build your understanding

Open one idea at a time. Compare the model with the common mix-up.

1Start, change, endpoint

For a+b, begin at a and move according to b. The endpoint is the sum.

Model: (-3)+(+8): start -3, move right 8, end +5.

Common mix-up: Starting at 0 and showing only the second addend.

2Direction and distance

The sign gives direction; magnitude gives the number of intervals.

Model: Adding -7 means move left/down 7.

Common mix-up: Counting tick marks rather than spaces.

3Vertical number lines

Temperature and elevation often use up as positive and down as negative.

Model: -8°C rising 13°C ends +5°C.

Common mix-up: Moving to +13 instead of moving 13 from -8.

4Match chip and line models

Cancelled pairs correspond to opposite movement that returns along the line.

Model: 5P/8N matches right 5 then left 8, ending -3.

Common mix-up: Matching only the final value and ignoring the two addends.

5Multiple changes

Follow each later addend from the previous endpoint and label every landing point.

Model: (+4)+(-9)+(+3): endpoints +4,-5,-2.

Common mix-up: Restarting from zero for each change.

WATCH THE THINKING

Worked examples

Rightward change(-3)+(+8)
  1. Start at -3.
  2. Move right 8 intervals.
  3. Land at +5.

Conclusion: +5

Leftward change(+6)+(-10)
  1. Start at +6.
  2. Move left 10.
  3. Land at -4.

Conclusion: -4

Same negative direction(-5)+(-7)
  1. Start -5.
  2. Move left 7.

Conclusion: -12

Three moves(+4)+(-9)+(+3)
  1. Begin +4.
  2. Move left 9 to -5.
  3. Move right 3 to -2.

Conclusion: Endpoints +4,-5,-2.
