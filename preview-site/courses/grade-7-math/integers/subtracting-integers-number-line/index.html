START HERE

The big ideas

I will show subtraction on a number line, rewrite it as addition of the opposite, and choose a model that fits the question.

Add the opposite

a-b has the same effect as a+(-b).

Keep the first integer

Change only the operation and the second integer’s sign.

Subtraction gives movement

Subtract positive moves left; subtract negative moves right.

Difference can mean two things

Directed change has a sign; distance is nonnegative.

LEARN

Build your understanding

Open one idea at a time. Compare the model with the common mix-up.

1Connect tiles and line

Removing a signed amount has the same effect as moving in the direction of its opposite.

Model: (-1)-(-4) leaves +3 and matches (-1)+(+4).

Common mix-up: Using the subtrahend’s original sign for the line direction.

2Keep, change, opposite

Keep the first integer, change subtraction to addition, and replace only the second integer by its opposite.

Model: (-7)-(+5)=(-7)+(-5).

Common mix-up: Changing both integers.

3Four cases, one rewrite

The same equivalence works for every sign combination; then use addition reasoning.

Model: (+4)-(-9)=(+4)+(+9)=+13.

Common mix-up: Memorizing “two negatives make positive” without locating the operation.

4Directed change and distance

Change from initial to final is final-initial and may be signed. Distance is the nonnegative separation.

Model: From +3 to -8: change -11, distance 11.

Common mix-up: Always doing larger minus smaller even when direction matters.

5Choose a useful model

Use chips to explain zero pairs, a line for movement, and symbols for efficient large calculations.

Model: From -85 to +40 is clearer as 40-(-85)=+125.

Common mix-up: Choosing a model but giving no evidence.

WATCH THE THINKING

Worked examples

Subtract positive(+8)-(+3)
  1. Rewrite (+8)+(-3).
  2. Start +8 and move left 3.

Conclusion: +5

Negative start(-6)-(+7)
  1. Rewrite (-6)+(-7).
  2. Move left 7.

Conclusion: -13

Subtract negative(+4)-(-9)
  1. Opposite of -9 is +9.
  2. Move right 9.

Conclusion: +13

Two negatives(-12)-(-5)
  1. Rewrite (-12)+(+5).
  2. Move right 5.

Conclusion: -7

Final minus initialTemperature rises from -15°C to +2°C.
  1. Use final-initial.
  2. (+2)-(-15)=(+2)+(+15).

Conclusion: +17°C
