START HERE

The big ideas

Use digit patterns to test 3, 6, and 9, find factors, and explain why zero cannot be a divisor.

Digit sums preserve remainders

For 3 and 9, each place-value power contributes the same remainder as its digit.

Six is an AND rule

A number is divisible by 6 only when it is divisible by both 2 and 3.

Rules reveal factor pairs

A successful test gives a divisor and its paired quotient.

Zero is not a divisor

No quotient makes 0 × q equal a nonzero dividend; 0 ÷ 0 also fails to name one unique quotient.

LEARN

Build your understanding

Open each idea, study the model, and compare it with the common mix-up.

1Divisibility by 3

Add the digits. If the sum is divisible by 3, the original number is too.

Model: 2,364 → 2+3+6+4=15 → divisible by 3.

Common mix-up: Do not inspect only the last digits.

2Divisibility by 9

Add the digits and test the sum for 9.

Model: 7,245 → 18 → divisible by 9 and therefore by 3.

Common mix-up: Divisible by 3 does not always imply 9.

3Divisibility by 6

Check evenness and the digit sum. Both tests must pass.

Model: 4,518 is even and its digit sum is 18.

Common mix-up: Passing either test alone is not enough.

4Division by zero

Division can be checked with multiplication. For a÷0=q, we would need 0q=a.

Model: 12÷0 is undefined because no q makes 0q=12.

Common mix-up: 0÷6=0 is different from 6÷0.

WATCH THE THINKING

Worked examples

TEST MANY RULESWhich rules work for 53,460?
  1. Digit sum 18 proves 3 and 9.
  2. Ending 0 proves 2, 5, and 10; 2 and 3 together prove 6.
  3. The final two digits, 60, prove divisibility by 4; the final three digits, 460, are not divisible by 8.

Conclusion: 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 9, and 10.

BUILD A NUMBERGreatest digit in 4□5 for divisibility by 9?
  1. Known digit sum is 9.
  2. The missing digit may be 0 or 9.
  3. Choose the greatest valid digit.

Conclusion: 495.

FACTOR PAIRSFind every factor of 84.
  1. Pair 1×84, 2×42, 3×28.
  2. Continue 4×21, 6×14, 7×12.
  3. Stop once factors cross.

Conclusion: 1,2,3,4,6,7,12,14,21,28,42,84.

REPAIR AN ERROR“24 ÷ 0 = 0.”
  1. Check the claim by multiplication.
  2. 0×0 is 0, not 24.
  3. No possible quotient works.

Conclusion: 24 ÷ 0 is undefined.
