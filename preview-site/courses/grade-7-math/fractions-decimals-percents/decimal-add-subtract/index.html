The big ideas

Align equal place values, not the ends of numerals.

Places must align

Tenths combine with tenths.

Estimation catches errors

A rounded estimate reveals misplaced decimals.

📘

LEARN

Build your understanding

1. Align decimal points

Equal places form vertical columns. Use the model 12.45+3.80=16.25. to keep the quantities and place values visible. Estimate or compare with a benchmark before accepting the result; this also exposes the common error of aligning final digits.

Model: 12.45+3.80=16.25.

Common mix-up: Aligning final digits.

2. Use placeholder zeros

Zeros can show missing places without changing value. Use the model 7.4=7.40. to keep the quantities and place values visible. Estimate or compare with a benchmark before accepting the result; this also exposes the common error of treating a placeholder as extra value.

Model: 7.4=7.40.

Common mix-up: Treating a placeholder as extra value.

3. Regroup by place

Trade one place for ten of the next smaller place. Use the model 8.00−2.37=5.63. to keep the quantities and place values visible. Estimate or compare with a benchmark before accepting the result; this also exposes the common error of subtracting without regrouping.

Model: 8.00−2.37=5.63.

Common mix-up: Subtracting without regrouping.

4. Interpret the operation

Total, remaining, and difference indicate equations. Use the model $20−$6.75=$13.25. to keep the quantities and place values visible. Estimate or compare with a benchmark before accepting the result; this also exposes the common error of answering a different question.

Model: $20−$6.75=$13.25.

Common mix-up: Answering a different question.

Worked examples

  1. Write 14.600+2.385.
  2. Add aligned columns.
  3. Check that 16.985 has a reasonable size and keeps the required unit or representation.

Conclusion: 16.985

Subtraction · 9−3.47
  1. Write 9.00−3.47.
  2. Regroup and subtract.
  3. Check that 5.53 has a reasonable size and keeps the required unit or representation.

Conclusion: 5.53

Context · 12.5 m−4.78 m
  1. Align hundredths.
  2. Subtract and attach metres.
  3. Check that 7.72 m has a reasonable size and keeps the required unit or representation.

Conclusion: 7.72 m

Check · 6.2+0.45
  1. Write 6.20+0.45.
  2. Compare with estimate 6.7.
  3. Check that 6.65 has a reasonable size and keeps the required unit or representation.

Conclusion: 6.65
