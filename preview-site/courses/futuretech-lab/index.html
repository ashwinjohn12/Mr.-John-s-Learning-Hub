YOUR TOOLKIT

What will you create with?

You will use different tools depending on the mission. You do not need experience before you begin.

⌨MakeCode

Build programs with blocks and grow into more advanced coding.

▦micro:bit

Turn code into buttons, lights, motion, games, and physical interactions.

⚙Robotics

Use Hummingbird kits, sensors, LEDs, and servos to make machines react.

🎮Game Design

Create playable experiences using micro:bit controls and your own rules.

⬡3D Design

Design accurate digital objects and prepare useful creations for printing.

YOUR PATHWAY

Five levels. One growing set of skills.

New students start at Level 1. Returning students continue from where they left off. Levels are mastery-based, not tied to a term.
