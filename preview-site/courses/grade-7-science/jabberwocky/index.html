Welcome, Pathfinder.

For centuries, humans searched the universe for another world like Earth. Then we found one with clouds, oceans, rivers, mountains, forests, deserts, ice, weather... and life.

The Jabberwocky Council for Exploration and Colonization—the JCEC—has been asked one enormous question:

Could Jabberwocky become a new home for humans?

YOUR MISSION

Explore. Investigate. Decide.

You will change teams and scientific jobs throughout the year. You may be sent to a different continent in every mission phase.

You will inherit evidence from earlier teams—and future scientists will inherit yours.

Your work does not disappear when your unit ends.
