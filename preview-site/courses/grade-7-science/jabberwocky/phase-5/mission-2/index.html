CLASS 1

Learn the Science

MINERAL naturally occurring Earth material with characteristic propertiesROCK material made from one or more mineral/material components

Rock classes make the most sense when you begin with how the rock formed. The pattern for this mission is:

EVIDENCE→PROCESS→ROCK CLASS
🔥IGNEOUS

Molten material cools and solidifies.

Possible clues: interlocking crystals, very fine texture, or holes from trapped gas. One clue alone is not proof.

🧱SEDIMENTARY

Sediments are deposited, then compacted and cemented.

Possible clues: layers, visible grains or fragments, rounded/cemented particles. Loose sediment is not yet sedimentary rock.

〰️METAMORPHIC

Existing rock is changed by heat and pressure without completely melting.

Possible clues: banding, aligned minerals, recrystallized texture or altered layers. Appearance alone may still be uncertain.

ONE CLUE ≠ PROOF

Layers, crystals, colour or holes can be useful evidence, but a single appearance clue may fit more than one history. Stronger explanations combine several observations and say what remains uncertain.

THE ROCK CYCLE IS A PROCESS NETWORK

Earth materials can take more than one path.

DO NOT MEMORIZE ONE CIRCLE
MELTING

Solid rock becomes molten material.

COOLING / SOLIDIFYING

Molten material becomes igneous rock.

WEATHERING / BREAKDOWN

Rock is broken into smaller material at or near the surface.

EROSION / TRANSPORT

Loose material is moved.

DEPOSITION

Moved sediment settles.

COMPACTION / CEMENTATION

Sediment becomes sedimentary rock.

HEAT / PRESSURE

Existing rock changes without fully melting, producing metamorphic rock.

Useful idea: any exposed rock can break down; sediments can be moved and deposited; buried rock can be changed by heat/pressure; rock can melt and later cool again. There is no single route every rock must follow.
Need Help? / Learn More

Mission 3 will investigate weathering, erosion and deposition in much more detail. Here they only help you understand possible rock-cycle pathways.

Radiometric dating, crystallography, mineral chemistry and advanced plate-tectonic mathematics are outside this required Grade 7 pathway.

LOCAL EARTH EVIDENCE · SOUTHERN ALBERTA

Calgary-area sand and gravel

Alberta Geological Survey source ↗

The Alberta Geological Survey reports major Calgary-area sand and gravel deposits associated with river and glacial-water processes, including alluvial deposits connected to the Bow and Elbow rivers. This is sediment evidence: it shows that transport and deposition can build layers of loose Earth material.

What can we infer?Moving water can transport and deposit sediment.What can we NOT infer from this alone?That every local sediment layer is already sedimentary rock, or that a Jabberwocky site formed the same way.

Investigate

MAIN INVESTIGATION

Rock Cycle Evidence Lab

5 EVIDENCE CASES

Question: What formation process is consistent with the evidence, which rock class fits, and what remains uncertain?

WHAT EVIDENCE?→WHICH PROCESS?→WHICH ROCK CLASS?→WHAT IS UNCERTAIN?
MATERIALS REALITY CHECK

Preferred: existing school rock samples if useful, hand lenses if already available, small sediment examples if already available, printed/projected evidence cards, process cards and pencils. Do not buy a rock collection for this mission.

APHOTO / OBSERVATION CARD

Coarse interlocking crystals

EVIDENCE
Several visible crystals fit together tightly. No layering or rounded sediment grains are visible.
POSSIBLE PROCESS
Slow cooling and solidifying could allow larger crystals to form.
CONSISTENT CLASS
IGNEOUS is consistent with the evidence.
UNCERTAINTY
The card does not identify a named rock or prove where the molten material cooled.
BPHOTO / OBSERVATION CARD

Layered cemented grains

EVIDENCE
Visible layers contain many sand-sized grains. Some grains are rounded and appear joined together.
POSSIBLE PROCESS
Deposition followed by compaction/cementation could explain the texture.
CONSISTENT CLASS
SEDIMENTARY is consistent with the evidence.
UNCERTAINTY
The card does not show how long deposition took or exactly where the sediment came from.
CPHOTO / OBSERVATION CARD

Banded altered texture

EVIDENCE
Light and dark mineral-rich bands run through the sample. Grains appear stretched or aligned rather than loose.
POSSIBLE PROCESS
Heat and pressure could change an older rock and rearrange/recrystallize minerals.
CONSISTENT CLASS
METAMORPHIC is consistent with the evidence.
UNCERTAINTY
The original rock type is not supplied.
DPHOTO / OBSERVATION CARD

Dark fine texture with holes

EVIDENCE
The sample is dark and very fine grained. Small rounded holes occur through part of the surface.
POSSIBLE PROCESS
Rapid cooling of molten material, with gas bubbles trapped as it solidified, could explain this evidence.
CONSISTENT CLASS
IGNEOUS is consistent with the evidence.
UNCERTAINTY
Colour alone is not useful enough to make the classification.
EPHOTO / OBSERVATION CARD

Fine dull sample with few clues

EVIDENCE
The sample is fine grained and dull. No clear layers, bands, large crystals or cemented particles can be confirmed from the available view.
POSSIBLE PROCESS
More than one formation history could produce a fine texture.
CONSISTENT CLASS
NOT ENOUGH EVIDENCE YET is the strongest conclusion.
UNCERTAINTY
A closer view, additional property tests or a better sample could change the interpretation.
TEAM TASK

For each case, cover or ignore the suggested interpretation first. Use only the evidence to choose a process card and a rock-class card. Then compare your reasoning with the supplied interpretation and identify where uncertainty remains.

NO-PURCHASE FALLBACKThe complete lab can run from the projected/printed cards above. Physical rock samples and hand lenses are optional supports, not requirements. No heating, melting, baking or chemical treatment is needed.
