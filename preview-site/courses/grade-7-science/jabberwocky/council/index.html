JCEC TRANSMISSION // FINAL SESSION

The specialist investigations are over.

Ecology told JCEC what living systems could be damaged. Plant science showed what living resources might support people and what sustainable use would require. Thermal science identified survival and energy demands. Structural science tested what safe construction would require. Geology revealed what the ground can—and cannot yet—tell us.

Now the Council must decide what level of human presence the evidence can actually support.

No continent has a predetermined correct answer. Different delegations may reach different conclusions if they use the evidence carefully and make their safeguards explicit.
