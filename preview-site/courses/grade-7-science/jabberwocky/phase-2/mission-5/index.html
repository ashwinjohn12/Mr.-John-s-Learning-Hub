CLASS 1

Learn the Science

Mission 5 adds very little new vocabulary. It asks you to use science you already learned to judge a bigger human plan.

🗺️LAND USE

Farms, plantations, roads and processing areas can change habitats, soil and water. More developed land usually means less natural land left unchanged.

💧PRODUCTION INPUTS

Irrigation, fertilizer and pest management can improve production, but each can also create unintended effects when overused or poorly placed.

🌾VARIETY & RESILIENCE

Productive systems still need enough variety and safeguards to cope with pests, unusual weather and changing conditions.

♻️SUSTAINABILITY

A sustainable system meets human needs without using resources or damaging ecosystems faster than they can recover.

Investigate

MAIN INVESTIGATION

Production Footprint Challenge

TEAM WORK

Question: Can we meet a human resource need and still protect the conditions the plant and ecosystem depend on?

  1. Open only your continent case. Read the production goal, six map zones and three constraints.
  2. Place a small set of planning tiles: growing/harvest, protected habitat, water access, road/infrastructure, processing/storage, buffer/protection.
  3. Build one plan that meets the goal while obeying all three constraints.
  4. When your teacher says REVEAL, open the consequence card.
  5. Revise exactly one part of the plan and explain why that change makes the system safer or more sustainable.
Keep the footprint small.This is a resource decision, not a city-building game. You need a defensible plan, not the most elaborate map.

CLASSES 3–4

Make a Decision

ONE TEAM QUESTION

What is the safest realistic way humans should use this plant resource?

🌿Limited monitored harvest

Use small amounts from established populations while monitoring recovery.

🌱Small managed cultivation

Grow the plant deliberately in limited farms, gardens, forestry plots or other managed areas.

🏗️Controlled-environment cultivation

Use protected, contained, greenhouse, raised-bed, wetland-compatible or soilless systems where appropriate.

⛔Do not develop yet

Current evidence or uncertainty makes development too risky.
