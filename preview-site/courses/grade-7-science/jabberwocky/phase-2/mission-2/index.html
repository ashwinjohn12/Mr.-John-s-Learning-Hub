CLASSES 1–3

Learn the Science

Six science words, but only three jobs to remember.

1
JOB 1

GET MATERIALS

Diffusion

Particles spread from an area where there are more of them toward an area where there are fewer.

Osmosis

Water moves across a cell membrane toward an area with less available water.

Plants use these kinds of movement as water and dissolved materials enter and move between cells.

2
JOB 2

MOVE WATER & MATERIALS

Conduction / transport

Water and dissolved materials move from roots through internal transport tissues toward stems and leaves.

Transpiration

Water leaves the plant from leaf surfaces as water vapour.

Water entering roots, moving upward and leaving leaves is part of one connected pathway.

3
JOB 3

MAKE FOOD & EXCHANGE GASES

Photosynthesis

Plants use light, water and carbon dioxide to make food. Oxygen is released.

Gas exchange

Carbon dioxide can enter leaves and oxygen can leave through tiny openings.

light + water + carbon dioxide → food + oxygen

MAKING THE NEXT GENERATION

🌰 Seed→🌱 Germination→🪴 Seedling→🌿 Mature plant→🌼 Flowers / seeds

Propagation means making new plants. Seeds are one method; some plants can also start from cuttings, runners or other plant parts.

Learn More: what are the plant's transport tubes?

Plants have specialized tissues that move water, minerals and sugars. You may hear the words xylem and phloem. For this mission, you only need the bigger idea: materials move through transport tissues inside the plant.

JCEC Growth Trial

TEAM WORK

Question: What changes when one important growing condition changes?

CONTROLNormal growing conditions
CHANGED CONDITIONYour teacher chooses one condition for the whole class

  1. Label two growing containers: CONTROL and CHANGED.
  2. Use the same seed type, number of seeds and growing medium in both.
  3. Give the control normal conditions. Change only the condition your teacher assigned in the second container.
  4. Place both containers where your teacher directs and record the starting setup.
  5. On observation days, spend only 3–5 minutes recording germination, height, leaf number and visible health.
Fair test:Change one condition. Keep the other important conditions as similar as possible.

Gyre · Frumious Basin

Skyroot

Cold temperatures and a short growing season make timing especially important.

1Water uptakeSkyroot grows in thin pockets of mountain soil that can dry quickly between rain or snowmelt.

GROWING CHALLENGE: Roots need access to enough liquid water even when conditions are cold.

2PhotosynthesisThe warm growing season is brief, but summer light can be strong.

GROWING CHALLENGE: The plant has limited time to capture light and make enough food for growth.

3GerminationSeeds remain inactive through the cold season and begin growing after soils warm.

GROWING CHALLENGE: Starting too early could expose young plants to freezing conditions.

4ReproductionSkyroot flowers open quickly during the warmest part of summer.

GROWING CHALLENGE: Humans would need a growing schedule that still allows flowering and seed production.

🌿
Brillig · Tulgey Station

Rainspout Tree

Brillig has abundant water, but light, drainage and nutrient movement can still limit growth.

1PhotosynthesisDense forest vegetation creates deep shade below the canopy.

GROWING CHALLENGE: Cultivated plants still need enough light to make food.

2Water uptakeHeavy rain keeps soil wet for long periods.

GROWING CHALLENGE: More water is not always better; growing areas need a way to avoid permanently flooded roots.

3TransportFrequent rainfall can move dissolved materials through shallow soils quickly.

GROWING CHALLENGE: Plants need water and dissolved materials to remain available to their roots.

4ReproductionRainspout Tree flowers and fruit are normally part of a busy canopy community.

GROWING CHALLENGE: A growing system must still allow successful flowering, pollination and seed production.

🏝️
Manxome · Calloo Island

Storm Palm

Storms, wind and salt exposure make reliable water balance difficult on the islands.

1Osmosis / water uptakeCoastal soils can receive salty spray during storms.

GROWING CHALLENGE: Too much salt can make it harder for roots to take in water.

2TranspirationStrong ocean winds move across exposed islands.

GROWING CHALLENGE: Wind can increase water loss from leaves even when the air is not very hot.

3GerminationYoung Storm Palms establish best in sheltered patches after storms.

GROWING CHALLENGE: Seedlings need enough fresh water and protection while roots develop.

4ReproductionFruits can float between islands.

GROWING CHALLENGE: Humans must plan how to grow the plant without accidentally spreading it into sensitive areas.

🏜️
Slithy Toves · Borogove Station

Reservoir Thorn

Water availability is the main challenge, and extra irrigation could also create salt problems.

1Osmosis / water uptakeFresh water is scarce and surface soil is often extremely dry.

GROWING CHALLENGE: Roots need enough available water for cells to stay hydrated.

2TranspirationHot days and intense sunlight can increase water loss from leaves and stems.

GROWING CHALLENGE: Humans would need to reduce unnecessary water loss.

3GerminationReservoir Thorn seeds often begin growing soon after rare rainfall.

GROWING CHALLENGE: Seeds need the right moisture at the right time.

4Water balanceRepeated irrigation can leave salts behind as water evaporates.

GROWING CHALLENGE: Adding more water could solve one problem while creating a new growing problem.

❄️
Wabe · Beamish Station

Ember Moss

Cold temperature and a very short growing season limit when growth and reproduction can happen.

1Water uptakeOnly a shallow surface layer of soil thaws during the growing season.

GROWING CHALLENGE: Roots can use liquid water only where the ground has thawed.

2PhotosynthesisSummer days are long, but temperatures stay cool.

GROWING CHALLENGE: Light may be available while low temperature still slows overall growth.

3GerminationEmber Moss seeds begin growing only after the surface layer warms.

GROWING CHALLENGE: A protected growing space could extend the time seedlings can grow.

4ReproductionThe plant flowers and produces seed rapidly during midsummer.

GROWING CHALLENGE: Any human growing plan must leave enough time for the whole life cycle.

🌾
Bandersnatch · Jubjub Station

Goldstem Grain

Conditions are favourable, but water timing and seasonal change still matter.

1PhotosynthesisOpen prairie provides strong light through a long growing season.

GROWING CHALLENGE: Light is rarely the main limitation for Goldstem Grain.

2Water uptakeFresh water is available, but windy periods can dry the upper soil.

GROWING CHALLENGE: Young plants need reliable moisture while roots are small.

3TranspirationPersistent prairie winds move air across narrow leaves.

GROWING CHALLENGE: Plants can lose water quickly during warm, windy periods.

4Germination / reproductionSeeds survive the cold season and germinate when spring conditions improve.

GROWING CHALLENGE: Humans need to match planting and harvesting to the seasonal life cycle.

🌲
Gimble · Galumph Station

Ironwood

Ironwood has access to water, but young trees compete strongly for light and grow slowly.

1PhotosynthesisDense forest canopy creates shaded ground below mature trees.

GROWING CHALLENGE: Young Ironwood needs enough light to make food and grow.

2TransportMature trees move water from large roots through tall woody stems to broad leaves.

GROWING CHALLENGE: A growing system must supply enough water for a large plant over many years.

3GerminationSeedlings establish most successfully in openings where light reaches the forest floor.

GROWING CHALLENGE: Planting location can affect whether a seedling survives its first years.

4ReproductionIronwood takes many years before it produces large numbers of seeds.

GROWING CHALLENGE: Slow reproduction means harvested trees cannot be replaced quickly.

🪷
Mimsy · Mome Station

Floatroot

Water is abundant, but waterlogged, nutrient-poor conditions create a different set of limits.

1Water uptakeFloatroot grows in saturated mud and shallow water.

GROWING CHALLENGE: Humans do not need to add much water, but they must keep water levels suitable.

2Photosynthesis / gas exchangeLeaves float or rise above the water surface.

GROWING CHALLENGE: Leaves need access to light and air even when roots are in flooded ground.

3TransportWetland soils contain relatively few available nutrients.

GROWING CHALLENGE: Plants still need dissolved materials for growth even when water is plentiful.

4ReproductionFlowers rise above the water and seeds can float to new areas.

GROWING CHALLENGE: A contained growing system may be needed if humans want to control where new plants establish.
