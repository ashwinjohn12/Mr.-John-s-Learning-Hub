CLASS 1

Learn the Science

⚖️STABILITY

How likely a structure is to stay in its intended position instead of tipping, sliding or collapsing.

⬇️MASS DISTRIBUTION + FOUNDATION

Where mass is located and how the structure is supported can change its stability.

🧱STRENGTHENING THE DESIGN

Shape, corrugation, lamination and better fastening or bracing can improve structural performance.

🛡️MARGIN OF SAFETY

A safe structure should handle more than the exact ordinary condition we expect.

CENTRE OF GRAVITY — HELPFUL IDEA, NOT A CALCULATION

Mass placed lower and more evenly can often improve stability because the structure is less likely to tip. You do not need to calculate a centre of gravity in Mission 4.

MARGIN OF SAFETY

Should a structure fail at exactly its normal expected load?

NORMAL USE
3 load units→SAFE DESIGN
more than 3

A safe design should tolerate conditions beyond ordinary expected use. JCEC is looking for a safety rule, not a mathematical safety-factor calculation.

Need Help? / Learn More

What is a foundation?

The foundation or support system transfers loads from the structure to the site below. Its job depends on the ground conditions and the structure above it.

Why strengthen by shape?

Changing shape, corrugating, laminating or bracing can change how a structural part handles forces without simply adding a large amount of material.

🚫 NO STRONGEST / TALLEST MODEL COMPETITION

There is no prize for holding the most mass. Your teacher sets a maximum safe test load. If your model reaches that maximum, stop testing. The evidence and explanation matter more than destruction.

MATERIALS REALITY CHECK

Index cards/cardstock, scrap cardboard, a small amount of tape, binder clips, coins/washers/small classroom masses and string if useful. No hot glue, knives, saws or large construction kits are required.

SAFETY LIMITS
  • Desk-sized models only.
  • Small classroom loads only.
  • Teacher-set maximum test load.
  • No snapping materials or throwing/dropping loads.
  • No tall structures above desk height.
