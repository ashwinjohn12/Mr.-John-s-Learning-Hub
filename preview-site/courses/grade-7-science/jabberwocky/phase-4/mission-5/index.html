CLASS 1

Learn the Science

Mission 5 does not add a large new vocabulary list. Instead, JCEC uses six review questions to decide whether a structural system deserves authorization.

🎯FUNCTION

Does the structural system do the job it is supposed to do?

🔁RELIABILITY

Can it perform consistently under the expected conditions?

🛡️SAFETY

Does it account for loads, stability and a margin of safety?

🧱MATERIAL EFFICIENCY

Does the idea use more structural material than it needs?

🧰CONSTRUCTION COST / COMPLEXITY

How demanding would the system be to build, maintain or modify?

🌱ENVIRONMENTAL IMPACT

Does it obey the ecological and site restrictions inherited from earlier teams?

RELATIVE COST / COMPLEXITY ONLY

Use simple labels such as LOW / MEDIUM / HIGH resource demand or 1 / 2 / 3 JCEC construction units. You are comparing ideas—not calculating real construction budgets or engineering economics.

Need Help? / Learn More

What is reliability?

Reliability asks whether a system can keep doing its job under the conditions it was designed for—not just whether it succeeded once.

What does material efficiency mean?

Using more material can sometimes improve performance, but good structural design tries to meet the requirement without wasting material or creating new problems.

CONCEPTUAL REVIEW

This is a design-review simulation, not a final construction project. Use evidence to assemble one structural concept, review it, then make one evidence-based revision.

STRUCTURAL FORM□ Frame□ Shell□ Frame + shell
SUPPORT APPROACH□ Distributed / wider support□ Limited / elevated support□ Compact support footprint□ Modular / lightweight approach
MATERIAL PROPERTIES□ Stiff□ Flexible where controlled movement helps□ Strong under tension□ Strong under compression□ Lightweight□ Durable
CONNECTIONS□ Fixed where movement should be limited□ Flexible where controlled movement is useful
STRENGTHENING□ Corrugation□ Lamination□ Component shape□ Bracing / improved fastening
PROPERTIES FIRST

Choose useful structural properties and behaviours. JCEC has not established that named materials such as steel or titanium are available on your continent.
