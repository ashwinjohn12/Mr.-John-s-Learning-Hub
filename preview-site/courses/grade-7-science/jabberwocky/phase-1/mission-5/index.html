CLASSES 1–2

Learn the Science

Start with four ideas.

📦Introduced species

An organism moved by humans to a place where it did not live before.

⚠️Invasive species

An introduced species that spreads and causes harm. Introduced does not automatically mean invasive.

🧪Pollutant

A substance released into the environment that can harm organisms or change conditions.

🛡️Conservation

Protecting species, habitats and ecosystem connections from serious harm.

To call an introduced species invasive, look for BOTH:1 · evidence it is spreading2 · evidence it is causing ecological harm
Three conservation words you will use

Endangered: at serious risk of disappearing.

Local extinction: a species disappears from one area but still exists elsewhere.

Global extinction: a species disappears everywhere.

CLASS 1

Stowaway Spread Simulation

TEAM WORK

Question: Can an early checkpoint reduce the spread of a hidden stowaway?

You need:6 labelled cups or zonesneutral counters4 different-coloured “stowaway” countersa small scoop
  1. Mix the neutral counters and 4 stowaways in the Cargo cup.
  2. Round A: move random counters Cargo → A → B → C → D → E. Do not inspect them. Count how many zones receive a stowaway.
  3. Reset everything.
  4. Round B: repeat the same transfers, but inspect each transfer first. Put visible stowaways into a quarantine cup.
  5. Compare the two rounds. Which system stopped spread earlier?
Big idea:Human movement can create pathways for spread. Early checks can interrupt those pathways.
