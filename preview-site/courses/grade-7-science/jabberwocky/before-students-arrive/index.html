5 · QUICK RECOVERY GUIDE

Protect the science, not the apparatus

Chromebooks fail

Project the page and use paper/notebooks. Keep the same team/continent.

Printing is unavailable

Project cards/sheets and copy only the needed table/headings onto blank paper.

A lab runs long

Stop once students have enough evidence. Use the next flex period or supplied/class data.

Materials are missing

Shared station → teacher demonstration → supplied data/evidence cards.

A student is absent

Use team/class/supplied evidence. Do not recreate an entire lab unless useful.

A class is lost

Protect the core investigation, one decision and Team Record; cut presentations/polish first.

A group loses its saved continent posting

Use the teacher roster and restore the assigned continent at that phase hub. The five phase keys remain separate.

Phase 3 Thermos Challenge equipment fails

Use staggered thermometers, a teacher test or the supplied Prototype/Final datasets. Preserve graphing, diagnosis, redesign and the JCEC Thermos Design Report.
