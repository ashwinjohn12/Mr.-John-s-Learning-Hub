START HERE

The big idea

Solving an equation means finding the value that keeps the left side and right side equal.

An equation behaves like a balance. To preserve equality, any change made to one side must also be made to the other. Models such as balance scales and algebra tiles make inverse operations visible: remove equal amounts from both sides, create and remove zero pairs, or divide both sides into equal groups until one variable is isolated.

Think about itWhy does subtracting 4 from both sides of x + 4 = 9 keep the equation true while subtracting 4 from only one side does not?

LEARN

Build your understanding

01

An equation has two equal sides

An equal sign states that the expression on the left has the same value as the expression on the right. An expression such as 3x + 7 has no equal sign.

Remember: Equal means same value, not “the answer comes next.”

02

The variable is an unknown value

To solve means to find a value for the variable that makes the equation true.

Remember: The goal is usually to isolate one variable.

03

Preserve equality

If you add, subtract, multiply, or divide one side of an equation, make the matching change to the other side.

Remember: Whatever you do to one side, do to the other.

04

Zero pairs remove additive terms

If x − 3 = 1, adding 3 to both sides creates a zero pair on the left and leaves x = 4.

Remember: Opposites combine to zero.

05

Equal sharing isolates multiplied variables

If 2x = 10, divide both sides into 2 equal groups. One x must equal 5.

Remember: 2x means 2 × x.

Simplify before you solve

Simplify before solving when needed

For 3n + 2n − 1 = 9, combine like terms first to get 5n − 1 = 9. Then use the balance idea.

Remember: Simplifying changes the form but keeps the same solution.

COMMON MIX-UPS

“The equal sign means calculate the left side.”

An equal sign compares two values. Both sides must represent the same amount.

“I can remove something from only one side.”

That breaks equality. A balance stays level only when matching changes happen on both sides.

“2x = 10 means x = 8.”

2x means two equal x-groups total 10, so equal sharing gives x = 5.

“I should solve before simplifying.”

When like terms appear on one side, simplifying them first often makes the balance much easier to see.

WATCH THE THINKING

Worked examples

Start with the two core examples. Open the next examples when you are ready to apply the same reasoning in a more complex situation.

Example 1 · Expression or equation?

Classify 2(x − 5), 5b = 15, and 3x − 5 = −2.

  1. Look for an equal sign.
  2. 2(x − 5) has no equal sign, so it is an expression.
  3. 5b = 15 and 3x − 5 = −2 each compare two equal expressions.
Answer: One expression; two equations

Example 2 · Remove the same amount

Solve x + 15 = 23.

  1. Picture both sides on a balanced scale.
  2. Remove 15 from the left to isolate x.
  3. Remove 15 from the right to preserve equality.
  4. 23 − 15 = 8.
Answer: x = 8
Ready for more? Open Examples 3 and 4

Example 3 · Zero pairs and equal sharing

Solve 2n − 1 = 9.

  1. Add 1 to both sides to create a zero pair: 2n = 10.
  2. Divide both sides into 2 equal groups.
  3. Each n-group is 5.
Answer: n = 5

Example 4 · Simplify before solving

Solve 3n + 2n − 1 = 9.

  1. Combine like terms: 5n − 1 = 9.
  2. Add 1 to both sides: 5n = 10.
  3. Divide both sides by 5.
Answer: n = 2

LESSON SUMMARY

  • An equation states that two expressions have equal value.
  • Solving means finding a value that makes the equation true.
  • Matching operations on both sides preserve equality.
  • Zero pairs and equal sharing make inverse operations visible.
  • Simplifying first can make an equation easier to solve.
Need more help?
  • Draw a vertical divider to separate left and right sides.
  • Use algebra tiles or paper unit marks for every step.
  • Say the inverse operation aloud before making a move.
  • Check that every move happens on both sides.
Want an extra challenge?
  • Solve the same equation using both a balance drawing and symbolic steps.
  • Create a one-step equation with a negative solution.
  • Write a real-life story for 3x + 2 = 20.
