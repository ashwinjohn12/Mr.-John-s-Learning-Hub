PAT-STYLE THINKING

Explain, transform, and justify

1 · Coordinate reasoning

A point is in Quadrant III, 4 units from the y-axis and 7 units from the x-axis. Name it and explain both signs.

Show a strong answer

(−4, −7). Quadrant III is left and down, so both coordinates are negative.

2 · Compare transformations

Explain one way a translation and reflection are alike and one way they differ.

Show a strong answer

Both preserve size and shape. A translation keeps orientation, while a reflection reverses orientation across a mirror line.

3 · Rotation reasoning

Why does the centre of rotation not need a prime label in this unit?

Show a strong answer

The centre is fixed, so its image is the same point as the original.

4 · Design test

A pattern repeats congruent tiles but contains small gaps. Is it a tessellation? Explain.

Show a strong answer

No. Repetition is not enough; a tessellation must cover the surface with no gaps and no overlaps.
