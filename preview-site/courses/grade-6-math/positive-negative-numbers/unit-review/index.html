PAT-STYLE THINKING

Explain, model, and choose a strategy

1 · Compare

Two Alberta winter temperatures are −14 °C and −6 °C. Which is warmer? Explain using a number line or distance from zero.

Show a strong answer

−6 °C is warmer because −6 is farther right on the number line than −14. It is the greater number.

2 · Add

A game score changes by −18, then +27. Find the total change and explain how magnitude helps.

Show a strong answer

The signs are different. 27 has the greater magnitude, so 27 − 18 = 9 and the result is +9.

3 · Subtract with meaning

Explain why (+4) − (−7) is greater than +4.

Show a strong answer

Subtracting a negative means removing negative value. On a number line, it moves 7 units right, so (+4) − (−7) = +11.

4 · Connect subtraction and addition

Rewrite (−12) − (+5) as addition and solve.

Show a strong answer

Keep −12 and add the opposite of +5: (−12) + (−5) = −17.
