PAT-STYLE THINKING

Explain, justify, and choose a strategy

1 · Compose and decompose

Show two different products equal to 72, then explain why 2 × 2 × 2 × 3 × 3 is its prime factorization.

Show a strong answer

For example, 8 × 9 and 6 × 12 both equal 72. The expression 2 × 2 × 2 × 3 × 3 also equals 72 and uses only prime factors, so it is the prime factorization.

2 · Powers

Explain the difference between 4³ and 4 × 3.

Show a strong answer

4³ means three factors of 4: 4 × 4 × 4 = 64. The expression 4 × 3 equals 12.

3 · Equal priority

Evaluate 36 ÷ 6 × 3 and explain why multiplication does not automatically happen first.

Show a strong answer

Multiplication and division have equal priority, so work left to right: 36 ÷ 6 = 6, then 6 × 3 = 18.

4 · Full expression

Evaluate 6 + 2 × (5 − 2)² and show one justified step at a time.

Show a strong answer

5 − 2 = 3, then 3² = 9, then 2 × 9 = 18, then 6 + 18 = 24.
