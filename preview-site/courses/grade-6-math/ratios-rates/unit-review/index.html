PAT-STYLE THINKING

Explain, justify, and connect

5.1

Percent is a relationship

A student says 25% of 80 is 25 because “25% means 25 out of 100.” Explain the error.

Show a strong answer

The 25 describes a relationship to a whole of 100, not a fixed amount. Since 25% is one-quarter, 25% of 80 is one-quarter of 80, which is 20.

5.2

Equivalent-ratio error

A student says 3 : 5 = 6 : 8 because they added 3 to both terms. Explain why the relationship changed and give the correct ratio with first term 6.

Show a strong answer

Equivalent ratios multiply or divide both terms by the same factor. Since 3 was doubled to 6, 5 must also be doubled to 10. The correct equivalent ratio is 6 : 10.

5.3

Fair comparison

Store A sells 3 notebooks for $6 and Store B sells 5 for $9. A student chooses Store A because $6 is less than $9. Why is that comparison incomplete?

Show a strong answer

The packages contain different numbers of notebooks. Compare the same amount using unit price: Store A is $2.00 per notebook and Store B is $1.80 per notebook. Store B is the lower unit price.

SYNTHESIS · 5.1 + 5.2 + 5.3

School Event Supply Challenge

A drink mix uses juice : water = 2 : 3. You need a batch with 12 cups of water. Juice costs $3 for 2 cups. Find the amount of juice needed, the cost per cup of juice, and the total juice cost. Then explain which Unit 5 ideas you used.

Show a strong answer

For 2 : 3 = j : 12, 3 was multiplied by 4, so 2 × 4 = 8 cups of juice. The unit price is $3 ÷ 2 = $1.50 per cup. Eight cups cost 8 × $1.50 = $12. Equivalent ratios found the needed amount; unit rate found cost per cup; both used the same proportional-thinking idea of keeping relationships equivalent.
