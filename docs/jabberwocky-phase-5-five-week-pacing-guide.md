## Implementation priorities by week

### Week 1 — Evidence before identification
**Days 1–5**

Protect:
- Earth Reference Evidence vs Jabberwocky Survey Evidence
- observation vs inference
- rock vs mineral
- relative property evidence
- three rock classes through formation processes

If time is lost: reduce the number of sample/evidence cases before reducing reasoning.

### Week 2 — Finish rock history and run the Surface Change model
**Days 6–10**

Protect:
- uncertainty in Mission 2
- BREAK → MOVE → SETTLE
- one changed variable in the fair test
- quantitative + qualitative evidence
- anomaly/discrepancy discussion

Flex Day 7 can absorb sample/classification recovery. Do not turn the Surface Change activity into an erosion contest.

### Week 3 — Complete checkpoint and move into the deep record
**Days 11–15**

Protect:
- Mission 3 checkpoint reasoning
- conditional prediction / model limitations
- strata, folds and faults as evidence
- qualitative crustal-movement patterns
- fossil context and stewardship
- relative sequence rather than absolute age

Flex Day 12 is the main data/checkpoint recovery day.

### Week 4 — Finish deep-past interpretation and begin the final handoff
**Days 16–20**

Protect:
- accumulated evidence + uncertainty
- no invented fossil/fault/hazard canon
- Geological Evidence Locker as memory support only
- known vs inferred vs uncertain
- geological handoff ≠ final settlement decision

Flex Day 17 is the best Planet Earth review / optional science-check slot.

### Week 5 — Complete the geological handoff
**Days 21–25**

Protect:
- JCEC Geological Evidence Packet
- 3 strongest evidence items
- one important uncertainty
- protect/monitor rule
- what JCEC should investigate next
- handoff status
- 3–5 sentence Council message

Use Days 22–25 for completion, absence recovery, optional individual assessment, gallery review, feedback and interruptions.

## Seven flex periods — purposeful uses

1. **Day 7 — sample/classification recovery**
2. **Day 12 — Surface Change Fair Test / Mission 3 checkpoint recovery**
3. **Day 17 — Planet Earth review / optional individual check / Mission 4 sequence recovery**
4. **Day 22 — Mission 5 Geological Evidence Packet completion**
5. **Day 23 — absence recovery / individual assessment**
6. **Day 24 — optional Geological Evidence gallery/review**
7. **Day 25 — final interruption/reteach/transition buffer**

## What to cut first if the calendar compresses

Cut in this order:
1. extra sample cases,
2. extra physical trials,
3. student presentations,
4. graph-drawing polish if a teacher-prepared graph can be interpreted instead,
5. gallery/review enrichment.

Do **not** cut:
- Earth/Jabberwocky evidence-source separation,
- the Mission 3 fair-test/data reasoning,
- Mission 4 relative-sequence reasoning,
- uncertainty,
- the five distinct Team Records,
- Mission 5 handoff distinction from the final Council.

## Low-material pacing rule

**ordinary classroom supplies → shared/reused equipment → printables/data fallbacks → specialty equipment only as optional enrichment**

The unit does not require:
- a large rock/mineral collection,
- Mohs kit,
- loose glass scratch plates,
- real fossils,
- stream table,
- geology model kits,
- rock saws,
- or specialty geology kits/equipment.

## Assessment timing

- **M1 Ground Evidence Card — formative**
- **M2 Rock History Profile — formative**
- **M3 Surface Change Forecast — stronger checkpoint**
- **M4 Deep Record Timeline — formative synthesis**
- **M5 JCEC Geological Evidence Packet — major synthesis**

Suggested numerical balance if needed:
- **30% M3**
- **55% M5**
- **15% optional individual Planet Earth science check**

Without the individual check: approximately **35% M3 / 65% M5**.
