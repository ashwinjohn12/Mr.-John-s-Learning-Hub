# Grade 7 Mathematics Unit 3 — Internal Build Specification

## Approved scope

**Unit:** Fractions, Decimals, and Percents
**Lessons:** 3.1 Fractions to Decimals; 3.2 Comparing and Ordering Fractions and Decimals; 3.3 Adding and Subtracting Decimals; 3.4 Multiplying Decimals; 3.5 Dividing Decimals; 3.6 Order of Operations with Decimals; 3.7 Relating Fractions, Decimals, and Percents; 3.8 Solving Percent Problems.
**Implemented Alberta outcomes:** N2 (decimal operations in problems), N3 (percent problems from 1% to 100%), N4 (relationships among positive fractions and terminating or repeating decimals), and N7 (compare and order positive fractions, decimals to thousandths, and whole numbers).

## Learning progression

The sequence begins by connecting division, fractions, and decimal notation; uses benchmarks and common representations to compare values; develops the four decimal operations with estimation and place-value models; applies order of operations; then unifies fractions, decimals, and percents before solving percent problems. Grade 6 place value, fraction equivalence, ratios out of 100, and decimal operations are diagnosed rather than assumed.

Each lesson uses two sessions of about 40 minutes. Session 1 core: readiness, Big Ideas, Understand 1–3, Examples 1–2, Explore phases 1–3, and practice 1–4. Session 2 core: remaining support as needed, practice 5–8, Check Yourself, and exit ticket. Explore phase 4+, extra examples, vocabulary writing, and Apply are consolidation or extension.

## Assessment and repair

Every lesson contains eight supported questions, a disjoint 30-question bank sampled as five questions, and a three-question exit ticket. Results are delayed until complete submission. Repair destinations use actual section titles. The review contains eight targeted stations, 20 mixed questions, ten repairs, a separate 40-question Unit Check sampled as ten questions with coverage from every lesson, and a five-stage **Market Day Mission** arcade. Explanations are recorded evidence and are never keyword graded.

## Accessibility and responsive requirements

All controls are keyboard operable with 44 px minimum targets; regenerated assessments announce the new set and focus question one; submitted answers lock; feedback is textual and not colour-only; interactive models include live transcripts; reduced motion is respected. At 760 px and below, multi-column tools collapse; at 390 px and below, sticky progress becomes static, controls fill available width, models remain scrollable only inside labelled regions, and signed/decimal text inputs use reliable parsing rather than restrictive numeric keyboards.
