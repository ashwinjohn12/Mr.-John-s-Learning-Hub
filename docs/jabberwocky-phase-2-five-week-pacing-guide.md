# Jabberwocky: Mission 2190 — Phase 2 Five-Week Pacing Guide

## THE LIVING RESOURCE — Plants for Food & Fibre

**25 × 45-minute periods**

- **18 core mission classes**
- **7 purposeful flex / review / assessment / interruption classes**
- Student pathway: **Resource → Growth → Growing Zone → Variety → Sustainable Use**

The pacing below matches the student-facing Phase 2 website. Flex days are intentionally placed where plant growth, laboratory timing, absences, and assessment are most likely to need breathing room.

## Growth Trial timing at a glance

The Mission 2 Growth Trial should run in the background for roughly **7–10 calendar days**. It should not become a daily assignment.

- **Before Day 4:** If possible, start one small teacher backup tray 2–3 days early.
- **Day 4:** Teams set up CONTROL + CHANGED containers. Use one class-wide changed condition (reduced water, mild salinity, reduced light, or another teacher-approved condition).
- **Days 5–7:** Spend only **3–5 minutes** per selected class on germination / height / leaf number / visible health.
- **Day 8 flex:** Use for an extra observation, delayed germination, absences, or plant-process reteach.
- **Days 9–11:** Continue quick observations while Mission 3 begins. Take the final or near-final measurement by Day 11 when practical.
- **Day 12:** Students use the Growth Trial as one piece of evidence in the Growing System Recommendation.
- **Day 13 flex:** Use as a final measurement / graph / recovery day if biological timing needs it.

### If the biology does not cooperate

**Poor germination:** Use the teacher backup tray, pooled class data, or a teacher-provided sample dataset. The learning target is interpreting evidence, not producing a perfect crop.

**Little difference between conditions:** Treat that as valid evidence. Ask whether the treatment was strong enough, whether more time was needed, and what the data can or cannot support. Do not invent a difference.

**Plants mature too slowly:** Use the strongest available observation and carry on. The growth trial supports the mission; it does not control the whole unit calendar.

## Purpose of the seven flex periods

The seven flex periods are not seven extra lessons. Use them according to need, in this priority order:

1. **Protect essential investigations and final products.**
2. **Recover absences and failed / delayed plant growth.**
3. **Reteach the science students are actually misunderstanding.**
4. **Complete the Mission 3 checkpoint or Mission 5 synthesis.**
5. **Use a short individual science check when an individual mark is needed.**
6. **Use gallery walks / presentations only if time remains.**
7. **Keep Day 25 available for normal school interruptions or transition.**

## Assessment load

Recommended formal grading load:

- **Mission 1 — Living Resource Profile:** formative / feedback only
- **Mission 2 — Plant Growth Requirements Card:** formative / feedback only
- **Mission 3 — Growing System Recommendation:** **30%** of Phase 2 formal assessment
- **Mission 4 — Plant Variety Decision Card:** formative / light feedback
- **Mission 5 — JCEC Living Resource Plan:** **55%** of Phase 2 formal assessment
- **Optional individual science check:** **15%**

If a separate individual mark is not required, the short science check can remain formative and the teacher can weight Mission 3 and Mission 5 locally. Avoid converting Missions 1, 2, and 4 into additional major assignments.

## Launch rule

If the calendar becomes compressed, protect this sequence:

**real plant structures → Growth Trial → soil / growing-system evidence → selective-breeding simulation → Production Footprint Challenge → JCEC Living Resource Plan**

Cut presentation polish, extra examples, additional trial rounds, and gallery walks before cutting the core science or the final evidence-based decision.
