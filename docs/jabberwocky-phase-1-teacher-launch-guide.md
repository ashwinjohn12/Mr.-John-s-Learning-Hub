# Jabberwocky: Mission 2190 — Phase 1 Teacher Launch Guide

## FIRST CONTACT — Interactions & Ecosystems

This guide matches the deployed five-mission student experience, Team Setup, central teacher Sheet, Teacher Workflow, Team Recovery, and the **25 periods / 18 core classes / 7 purposeful flex classes** implementation plan.

Student sequence:

**Mission 1 — Explore Your Environment → Mission 2 — Meet a Native Species → Mission 3 — Build the Ecosystem → Mission 4 — Watch the Ecosystem Change → Mission 5 — Humans Have Arrived**

## Before Day 1 — current digital sequence
- Open the teacher workbook and confirm **Setup → Collection Status = Active**.
- Plan teams of 3–5 and continent distribution, but **Do not pre-create Team IDs** and do not assign continents before Team Setup.
- Students create and save the Team Profile with first names only. The system generates the stable Team ID.
- Confirm one row per team in **Team Directory** and **Team Profile ✓** in Mission Progress.
- Teacher assigns one continent after the team is registered; students save the continent on the same Chromebook and keep it for Missions 1–5.
- Student Save buttons store locally first and send the teacher copy automatically. A separate submission portal is not part of the normal workflow.
- Bookmark the teacher-directed Team Recovery page for Chromebook changes.

## MISSION MATERIALS MASTER LIST
Use **shared stations, reused classroom materials and printed/printable records** before buying or duplicating specialized kits.
- M1: shared thermometer(s), **one field sheet per team**, clipboards, string/markers; optional metre stick/light/moisture tools.
- M2: per team for Class 1 — **1 spoon, 1 pair of tweezers, 1 clothespin/tongs, 1 pair of chopsticks, 36 identical plastic counters, 1 collection cup, 1 timer, 1 flat tray, 1 empty 6-well egg carton and about 1 m of loose yarn**; **one two-page Mission 2 packet per team (lab + Native Species Card)**. Set 12 counters in each zone. Use 20-second trials and reset to 12 between structures. No separate reconstruction sheet is needed.
- M3: **reusable laminated Earth organism-card sets**, yarn/string, arrow markers; **one consumable Ecosystem Map per team**.
- M4: **reusable laminated/sleeved 5×5 sampling page**, counters, dry-erase marker; **one consumable Ecosystem Change Record per team**; shared/digital local source pack.
- M5: **reusable laminated six-zone Stowaway mat**, neutral + contrasting markers; one teacher pollutant/biomagnification model set; **one consumable JCEC Recommendation Board per team**.

### Final printing standard
- There are **no required per-student Mission 1–5 packets**.
- Initial setup is **9 sheets per team** across the whole phase, including three pages that should become reusable class-set materials.
- Once the M3 cards, M4 sampling page and M5 spread mat are laminated/reused, the ongoing load is **6 consumable sheets per team across all of Phase 1**.
- Do not print separate M2 reconstruction sheets, repeated M3 cards, repeated M4 grids, repeated M5 spread mats, a pollutant worksheet, an M5 evidence-summary worksheet, or full local-source packs for every student.
- Day 22 is the only planned individual print, and only if a clean individual science check is actually needed.
- Full details: `docs/jabberwocky-phase1-final-materials-plan.md`.

## Assessment map
- M1 Environment Profile — **FORMATIVE** observation/inference.
- M2 Native Species Card — **FORMATIVE** adaptation/evidence.
- M3 Ecosystem Map + compact report — **FORMATIVE** connections/cycles.
- M4 Ecosystem Change Record — stronger **CHECKPOINT**, formative or lightly graded.
- M5 JCEC Recommendation Board — **MAJOR SYNTHESIS**.
- Day 22 individual science check — optional clean individual evidence.
If percentages are required: Recommendation Board **55%**, short individual science check **30%**, selected individual reasoning/field evidence **15%**.

## Spreadsheet monitoring
- **Team Directory:** current team identity, first names/roles, Team ID, continent.
- **Mission Progress:** primary daily dashboard; Team Profile + M1–M5 = 6 checkpoints.
- **Missing Records:** catch-up/absence/final cleanup.
- **Intake:** troubleshooting/history, not the everyday grading screen.
- **Class Summary:** class-level completion.
- **Teacher Workflow:** operational troubleshooting.
- **25-Period Plan:** detailed daily prep, materials, projection, science/task, checkpoint, teacher Sheet check, cut/move decision and assessment use.

## Contingencies
- **Lab runs long:** protect the science conclusion and Save; move polish/repeats to flex.
- **A class is lost:** absorb work first into Days 15, 20, 23, 24, then 25; Day 24 is the first full activity to drop.
- **Outdoor work cancelled:** use the indoor fallback or move fieldwork to Day 15.
- **Device failure:** keep paper/physical work; if switching Chromebooks for the team, use Team Recovery with the existing Team ID.
- **Student misses a lab:** use team data + a short individual interpretation instead of recreating the whole lab.
- **Materials are limited:** rotate shared stations and use reused/printable substitutes.
- **Students overwhelmed:** reduce to the strongest evidence/one clear relationship before adding complexity.

## Absence/network/device recovery
Offline students still click Save. Local browser storage is the first copy. Do not clear browser/site data. When online, reopen Phase 1 and let the retry queue send; Save once more if needed. For a different Chromebook use **Team Directory → existing Team ID → Team Recovery**. Previously centrally sent work remains safe; old-device-only work does not transfer automatically.

## End-of-Phase-1 transition gate
Before clearing a team:
- Team Directory identity + continent correct.
- Mission Progress = **6/6 Complete**.
- Missing Records has no unresolved required checkpoint.
- Recommendation Board collected/assessed as intended.
- Any required individual evidence/feedback resolved.
- Class Summary complete count matches the teams actually being cleared.

## Teacher readiness audit
- **Do I know what to prepare tomorrow?**
- **Do I know what students should finish?**
- **Do I know what to assess?**
- **Can I recover if time is lost?**
- **Do teacher and student systems match?**

If all five answers are yes, teach the next core class rather than adding more system complexity.
