# Jabberwocky Phase 1 — Final Printable & Physical Materials Plan

## Purpose
This is the final classroom-launch materials plan for **Phase 1 — First Contact: Interactions & Ecosystems**. It reconciles the corrected Mission 1–5 print systems with the 25-period implementation plan.

Use the smallest practical amount of paper. Default to **one team copy**, **reusable class-set materials**, and **Chromebook-first evidence** unless a paper artifact is the intended Team Record.

## Final printing rule
- **Per student:** no required Mission 1–5 packet. Individual printing is optional only for Day 22 or a teacher-chosen reflection/check.
- **Per team:** print only the consumable Team Record / field sheet needed for that mission.
- **Reusable/laminated:** manipulatives, cards, grids and simulation mats that do not need to be collected for assessment.
- **Class set / teacher demo:** source cards and model cards/tokens that can be projected or shared.
- **Digital only:** continent evidence, graphs, incident evidence, pollutant data, Teacher Report Copy and central checkpoints unless the teacher deliberately chooses a paper fallback.

## Mission-by-mission final plan

### Mission 1 — Explore Your Environment
**Print per team**
- 1 × Earth Environmental Survey field sheet — consumable, Day 2.

**Do not print by default**
- Environment Profile. Complete/save digitally unless a paper fallback is needed.
- Story/background pages.

**Shared physical materials**
- clipboard
- pencil
- thermometer access
- string/markers
- optional metre stick / light / moisture tools

**Chromebook dependency**
- Day 1 Team Setup + continent save
- Day 3 continent evidence + Environment Profile

### Mission 2 — Meet a Native Species
**Print per team**
- 1 × two-page Mission 2 packet:
  - Page 1 — Survival Structures Lab
  - Page 2 — Native Species Card

**Important consolidation**
- **Do not print a separate reconstruction sheet.** Students reconstruct/draw directly on the Native Species Card, matching the live mission.

**Per-team physical materials for Class 1**
- 1 spoon
- 1 pair of tweezers
- 1 clothespin/tongs
- 1 pair of chopsticks
- 36 identical plastic counters
- 1 collection cup
- 1 timer
- 1 flat tray
- 1 empty 6-well egg carton
- about 1 m of loose yarn

**Standard zone setup**
- Open Ground — 12 counters spread across the flat tray
- Narrow Spaces — 2 counters in each of the 6 egg-carton wells
- Tangled Cover — 12 counters mixed through loose yarn
- Trial time — 20 seconds; reset the zone to all 12 counters before changing structures

**Chromebook dependency**
- continent assignment
- four First Contact clues
- final digital Team Record/save

### Mission 3 — Build the Ecosystem

- Print the Earth String Food Web organism-card page once per team set.
- Cut, laminate and store the cards for reuse.

**Print per team**
- 1 × Ecosystem Map page — consumable Team Record.

**Later years / future classes**
- Print **Page 2 only** once reusable card sets exist.

**Shared physical materials**
- laminated organism cards
- short yarn/string
- arrow markers
- pencils

**Chromebook dependency**
- continent organisms and feeding evidence
- change prompt

### Mission 4 — Watch the Ecosystem Change
**Reusable/laminated per team set**
- 1 × Population Sampling page (5×5 grid + sampling table) in a sleeve or laminated.
- Use dry-erase marker for sampled squares, counts, estimate and short reasoning because this is formative and does not need to be retained as the final Team Record.

**Print per team**
- 1 × Ecosystem Change Record page — consumable final Mission 4 record.

**Class set / teacher-curated**
- Local Earth-connection source cards. Share among teams or provide digitally; do not print one full source pack per student.

**Shared physical materials**
- 30–50 counters/beans/paper markers per team
- optional calculator/randomizer
- dry-erase marker if grid is laminated

**Chromebook dependency**
- continent graphs + field notes
- recovery timeline
- local source access if digital
- final digital Team Record/save

### Mission 5 — Humans Have Arrived
**Reusable/laminated per team set**

**Print per team**

**Teacher demo / shared model**

**Team simulation kit**

**Chromebook dependency**

- X-17 pollutant path

## Team-kit rule
Prepare reusable materials by **maximum number of simultaneous teams**, not by number of students.

Recommended team kit label:
- Team Kit 1
- Team Kit 2
- Team Kit 3
- etc.

Each kit can carry reusable counters/cards/tools from one mission to the next rather than creating separate complete sets for every student.

## Chromebook fallback rule
Do not pre-print full digital evidence packets “just in case.” Keep a small emergency stack of blank paper and use projected/teacher-shared evidence if a device fails. Paper artifacts already completed by the team remain usable; Team Recovery handles identity when a team changes Chromebook.

## Final duplication decisions
Remove/avoid:
- separate Mission 2 reconstruction sheet
- per-student copies of team records
- repeated Mission 3 card printing after laminated sets exist
- repeated Mission 4 grid printing after laminated sets exist
- repeated Mission 5 spread-mat printing after laminated sets exist
- pollutant/biomagnification worksheet
- separate Mission 5 evidence-summary worksheet
- full printed local-source pack for every student
- automatic printed review packet for Days 21–23

## Classroom launch standard
A teacher should be able to run Phase 1 with:
1. one reusable mission-material bin system,
2. six consumable sheets per team across the full phase once reusable materials are built,
3. Chromebooks for evidence and central records,
4. one optional individual assessment print only when it adds needed evidence.

If a new printable does not replace a real classroom friction point or required student record, do not add it.
